Color Article

Visual Branding and Interface Observations

Target's use of red

Target's bold red branding works well because it instantly grabs attention and establishes a clear visual identity. The striking contrast between the vibrant red and the neutral background ensures that the logo and important elements stand out and are easily identifiable. This powerful visual approach contributes to the brand's sense of confidence and makes it highly memorable to consumers.

Visit Target website

Concerns with the current screenshot

The screenshot under review appears to be overly saturated and depends excessively on yellow hues. Such a color scheme can give the design an outdated appearance and may diminish clarity for users, particularly when there is an absence of well-defined neutral tones to balance the palette. Adopting a more harmonious and balanced color approach would enhance readability significantly and contribute to a more refined and professional overall look.

Steam's muted tones

Steams approach to interface design

Steam preserves a tidy and uncluttered interface by employing subdued colors and a well-defined visual hierarchy. The search bar is straightforward to use and manage, while the selected color scheme steers clear of overly bright or harsh tones, yet still offers sufficient contrast to facilitate smooth navigation. This carefully controlled palette not only enhances usability but also contributes to a more contemporary and polished user experience, making the platform feel both accessible and up-to-date.
