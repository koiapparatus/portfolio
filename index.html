A BIT ABOUT ME

From building structures to building for the web.

At 46, I’ve recently started learning to code, transitioning from a long career in construction and other physically intensive roles. After two shoulder surgeries and two back surgeries, I’m working toward a more sustainable, less physically demanding career in web development.

In 2021, my family and I relocated from Seattle, Washington, to the Dallas area in Texas. I’ve always appreciated the outdoors and nature, and I’m enjoying all that life in Texas has to offer.

I’m developing my skills through the ed2go Web Design Professional program. This portfolio showcases what I’ve learned so far and the progress I’m making across design and development.
