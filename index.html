About Me

Hello, I'm Justin.

At the age of 46, I have recently started learning to code, transitioning from a long career in construction and other physically intensive roles. Having undergone two shoulder surgeries and two back surgeries, I am now aiming to build a more sustainable and less physically demanding career in web development.

In 2021, my family and I relocated from Seattle, Washington, to the Dallas area in Texas. I have a strong appreciation for the outdoors and nature, and I am fully embracing the lifestyle and experiences that living in Texas has to offer.

I am actively developing my web development abilities by completing various courses. This portfolio showcases the knowledge I have gained up to this point, as well as the advancements I am making in both design and development aspects. It reflects my ongoing commitment to improving my skills and growing as a web developer.
